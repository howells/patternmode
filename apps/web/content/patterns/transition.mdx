## Motion Presets

Four built-in presets — each bundles transition timing AND drag physics:

| Preset | Duration | Easing | Drag Stiffness |
|--------|----------|--------|----------------|
| snappy | 240ms | ease-out | 600 |
| smooth | 300ms | ease-in-out | 350 |
| bouncy | spring | stiffness 1200 | 400 |
| reduced | 10ms | linear | 1000 |

## Drag Dismissal

Content can be dismissed by dragging past a distance threshold (100px) or velocity threshold (500px/s). Uses logarithmic resistance — initial movement is free but gets progressively harder.
